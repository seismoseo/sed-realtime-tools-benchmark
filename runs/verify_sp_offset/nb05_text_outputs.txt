################ cell 34 [21] # 9. take-home messages, written from the computed numbers
[markdown]
**Take-home messages**

- **The hand picks now have exact UTC times.** The tables store seconds after the reference time of the server B SAC files, an earlier HYPOINVERSE origin (e.g. 2021-08-27 22:03:23.170 for the $\mathit{M}_{\mathrm{L}}$ 2.2 event); the SAC headers and the absolute April picks agree within 3.1 µs. The reference of the local waveform copy would have made every hand pick 20-181 ms late (section 1.1).
- **The S-P times are shorter than kim1983 predicts, in every picker.** Median offset -0.273 s for the hand picks (95% CI -0.304 to -0.239, 299 pairs), -0.262 s for PhaseNet+ and -0.306 to -0.254 s for the four deep-learning pickers (Figures C, D). Figures A and B show it on the waveforms: the S energy starts before the model S time.
- **About the same in every event.** The per-event medians of the hand picks lie between -0.311 and -0.239 s (Figure D).
- **Not exactly constant with distance.** -0.225 s within 0-30 km, -0.432 s at 60-70 km (around the direct/head-wave crossover) and -0.292 s at 70-101 km; station medians vary from -0.53 to -0.05 s (Figure C).
- **Vp/Vs 1.73 still fits the slope.** The origin-time-free Wadati fit gives Vp/Vs 1.728 (95% CI 1.718-1.736) for the hand picks and 1.727 for PhaseNet+; the data run parallel to the model line, shifted by about -0.29 s (Figure E a, b).
- **A constant is needed; a depth change or one Vp/Vs is not enough.** RMS: model as is 0.310 s; one uniform Vp/Vs 0.146 s; depth only 0.252 s even with a free depth per event; Vp/Vs 1.73 + constant 0.129 s (Figure E d).
- **Where the constant comes from cannot be told from S-P times.** A lower Vp/Vs only near the stations (Vp/Vs 1.660 above 5 km, RMS 0.130 s) or only around the source (Vp/Vs 1.650 at 10-15 km, RMS 0.133 s) fits as well as the constant. The kim2011 model, whose upper crust has a lower Vp/Vs, fits with nothing adjusted: offset -0.003 s, RMS 0.129 s. So the '0.27 s' belongs to kim1983 with Vp/Vs 1.73, not to every model (section 1.3, Figure E c).
- **Absolute locations that use S are biased, and the station geometry decides where the bias goes.** With all stations, the S picks as picked make the origin time 0.111 ± 0.005 s earlier than with S picks corrected by +0.273 s, while the depth changes by only +0.072 ± 0.047 km. With only the stations within 35 km (median 7 P and 9 S picks), the hypocenter comes out 1.61 ± 0.01 km shallower (11 of 11 events shallower) and the origin time 0.096 ± 0.006 s later (Figure F).
- **Double-difference relocation is unaffected.** pyrtdd gives identical positions and origin times with and without the S shift (largest differences 0.000 mm and 0.000 ms; Figure G): a delay common to one station and phase cancels in a difference of differences.
################ cell 35 [22] # 9. caveats and what to do, written from the computed numbers
[markdown]
**Caveats**

- **Not a PhaseNet+ artifact, but possibly a shared picking convention.** Hand and PhaseNet+ picks agree closely (medians -0.002 s for P and -0.013 s for S) and show the same offset, and so do four other pickers. All of them could still put the S onset at the same early feature of the waveform, so a picking convention shared by hand and machine picks is not excluded.
- **The cause cannot be located along the path.** S-P times measure only the total along each path. A low Vp/Vs near the source, a low Vp/Vs under the stations, and a constant picking delay all shorten S-P by a similar amount at every station (Figure E).
- **The size of the offset depends on the reference model.** It is -0.273 s against kim1983 with Vp/Vs 1.73 (HypoDD and the grids), -0.360 s against kim1983 P speeds with HYPOINVERSE's own S speeds (3.40/3.79/4.58 km/s), and -0.003 s against kim2011. The reference HYPOINVERSE locations (kim1983 run) therefore used S picks whose S-P times are about 0.36 s shorter than its own model predicts.
- **Station elevations.** The grids include the real station elevations; HYPOINVERSE and HypoDD used sea level. With stations at sea level the offset is -0.260 s instead of -0.273 s.
- **Layered-model artifact in Figure F.** 19 of 132 NonLinLoc depths sit at the 15 km layer boundary, where the misfit of a layered model has a kink; there the depth does not move freely, so the depth shifts of those solutions (mostly all-station runs with S as picked) may be underestimated.
- **The distance dependence is not perfectly flat** (larger offsets around the direct/head-wave crossover and beyond it), and the station medians scatter with a standard deviation of 0.11 s (stations with at least 3 pairs), so a single constant is a first-order description only.
- **What an analyst should do for absolute locations with S picks:** use S station corrections (e.g. NonLinLoc LOCDELAY or HYPOINVERSE station delays from the station medians of Figure C, file `station_median_offsets_hand.csv`), or at least one fitted S-P constant; or use a model with a lower Vp/Vs near the source or under the stations (kim2011 already behaves this way); and check depths against P-only locations, especially with few nearby stations. Double-difference relocation needs no change.
